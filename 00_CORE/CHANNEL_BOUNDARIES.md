# Channel Boundaries

## In scope
- Industrial failures
- Engineering failures
- Structural failures
- Maritime disasters
- Infrastructure failures
- Dams and water infrastructure
- Mining and workplace disasters when the decision-chain lens is strong
- Transportation/aviation when the story fits the investigation method

## Out of scope by default
- Generic breaking-news commentary
- Graphic compilation channels
- Pure engineering explainers with no failure story
- Generic true crime
- Scambaiting
- Reused-content compilations
- Topics whose evidence cannot support the claimed chain

Expansion rule:
A new subject class enters only if the same editorial method remains obvious to a cold viewer.

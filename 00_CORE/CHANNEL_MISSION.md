# Channel Mission

Build a durable documentary channel around catastrophic failures and the chain of decisions that made them possible.

The channel is not a generic disaster channel, true-crime channel, engineering tutorial channel, or news channel.

Every episode must answer, through evidence:

> How did a chain of decisions, warnings, mistakes, and system conditions turn a manageable situation into a catastrophe?

The chain is the protagonist. Never manufacture a villain to simplify a complex failure.

# Editorial Promise

Reconstruct the chain of decisions, warnings, mistakes, and system failures that turned a situation into a catastrophe.

## Signature ideas
- The Warning
- The Decision
- The Point of No Return
- The Reckoning
- The Legacy

## Default emotional contract
Facts create dread. The narrator does not perform dread.

The viewer should leave with competence satisfaction: they understand something about how the failure happened and what changed afterward.

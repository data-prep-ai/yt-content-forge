# Research Protocol

1. Start from the subject database and known demand evidence.
2. Prefer primary/government/investigation sources for load-bearing claims.
3. Build a timeline before writing narrative.
4. Identify every decision, warning, consequence, and investigation finding.
5. Separate `FACT`, `DOCUMENTED CLAIM`, `DISPUTED CLAIM`, and `UNKNOWN`.
6. Never fill a gap with model knowledge when the evidence pack is silent.
7. Verify rights status before using external visual assets.
8. Research is not complete until a claim ledger exists.

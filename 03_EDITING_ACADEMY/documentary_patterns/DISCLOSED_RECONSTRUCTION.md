# Pattern: DISCLOSED_RECONSTRUCTION

## Purpose
Manufacture the footage history never recorded — and label it honestly. The industry's contested fault line: Brick Immortar declares "ZERO AI tools"; Trace the Failure and Meridian Zero disclose. Our standard: reconstructions disclosed as illustrative, never passed off as archival (Bible §6.4, honesty gate).

## When to use
- Where no footage or photography exists of the event (night sinkings, internal failures, 1928 breaches).
- For the cold-open image when no archival image is legible enough.
- Sparingly: reconstruction is a supplement to evidence, not a replacement for it.

## When NOT to use
- Where archival material exists (reconstruction must never displace real evidence).
- Undisclosed, ever (the brand-killing risk — competitors brand against it explicitly: "No AI / Human Narrated").
- As the video's dominant visual language without disclosure in the description AND on screen.
- For document evidence (never fabricate a memo/report visual — see DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE).

## Narrative situation
Any beat where the record is visual-dark: the breach at night, the sinking in a storm, the internal mechanism (as an alternative to MECHANISM_CUTAWAY).

## Visual sequence
1. On-screen label: "Dramatized reconstruction" / "Illustrative reconstruction" — visible, not buried [per Meridian Zero's "visibly labelled" practice — OBSERVED].
2. The reconstruction plays in the established visual grammar (same color discipline, same restraint).
3. Cut back to evidence (archival, diagram, document) — the reconstruction never runs long enough to become the video's reality.

## Typical duration
5–20 seconds per reconstruction beat. Brief and labeled. The reconstruction illustrates a moment; it doesn't reenact the video.

## Motion strategy
Cinematic but restrained — the reconstruction should feel like an illustration that moves, not a film scene. No shaky-cam, no dramatic angles for their own sake.

## Transition strategy
Hard cut in from the label; hard cut out to evidence. The label is the transition's honesty.

## Sound strategy
[RESEARCH GAP.] Conservative: no Foley designed to thrill (no Hollywood creaks/explosions). The reconstruction is illustrative — its sound should be too, or silent.

## Common mistakes
- Undisclosed AI footage passed off as archival (credibility death in this genre — the observed bright line).
- Reconstruction as the dominant language (then it's not a documentary with reconstructions, it's a reenactment with claims).
- The AI look in thumbnails without care (garbled AI text is an observed slop tell).
- Disclosing in the description but not on screen (the viewer watching never reads the description — label the frame).

## Chain-of-Failure application
Our pipeline is AI-assisted, which makes this pattern load-bearing: every synthetic visual gets the on-screen label, every time, no exceptions. The standard is "would a viewer ever mistake this for archival?" — if yes, the label failed. Disclosed reconstruction lets us show the unshowable (the breach, the sinking, the mechanism) without ever lying about what the viewer is seeing.

# Pattern: HOOK_DATED_PARADOX

## Purpose
Open the video at the catastrophe's coordinates — exact date, the confident system, the catastrophe — in three flat sentences. The gap between the system and the catastrophe is the entire retention device.

## When to use
Every video. This is the mandatory cold open (Bible §1). Never deviate without a documented reason.

## When NOT to use
Never skip it. Variants allowed (named-human anchor, in-medias-res primary audio), but the date-first paradox is the default — exact-time anchoring appears in 8/10 top outliers [OBSERVED].

## Narrative situation
0:00–0:20. The viewer knows nothing. Establish *when* and *what's wrong*. Never establish *who the narrator is*.

## Visual sequence
1. The single most legible image of the subject (archival photo or disclosed reconstruction) — one image, no montage [per creative report §14].
2. Hold it while the three sentences land. The image must be instantly nameable (the 1-second read starts here).

## Typical duration
20 seconds for the paradox; stakes + promise to 1:00.

## Motion strategy
Slow push-in on the single image [INFERRED — genre norm]. No fast cuts in the first 20 seconds; the flatness of the delivery needs visual stillness to land.

## Transition strategy
Hard cut from the paradox image into the stakes/timeline graphic. No dissolve, no flourish — the cut itself is the rewind signal.

## Sound strategy
[RESEARCH GAP.] Conservative: near-silence or low bed under the three sentences; the toll beat that follows gets no stinger (Bible restraint gate).

## Common mistakes
- Asking the viewer a question in the first 30 seconds (measured: rhetorical questions are 0–3.6% of narration; the packaging asks, the narrator answers).
- Introducing the narrator or channel ("welcome back" = the documented amateur move).
- Opening chronologically at the beginning (zero winners do this).
- Montage cold opens — one legible image beats five fast cuts.

## Chain-of-Failure application
The paradox is always *system vs catastrophe*: the confident system in one line (the Bureau's newest dam; the unsinkable ship; the standard procedure), the catastrophe in one line. The implied question is never "what happened" — it is "how did those decisions connect?"

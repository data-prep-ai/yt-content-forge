# Pattern: REHOOK

## Purpose
Re-open the central question in the final third (65–80% runtime) — new evidence, the reversed theory, the vindicated warner placed late. A required beat, not an accident (Bible §4.5). The catastrophe is over; the viewer's attention needs a new question.

## When to use
Every episode, at 65–80%. The re-hook takes one of three forms (all observed):
1. **The question restated** — "Why Did She Sink? — The Theories" (the mystery returns).
2. **The reversal** — new evidence overturns the working theory (the 2020 ROV hole).
3. **The vindicated warner** — the person who was right, placed late (Chen Xing at 85%).

## When NOT to use
- Before 60% (it's not a re-hook if the hook never left — early placement is just structure).
- More than one re-hook (stacking re-hooks reads as the video not trusting its own story).
- A manufactured question the video doesn't answer (the re-hook must pay off before the legacy close).

## Narrative situation
Post-catastrophe, pre-reckoning (template ~10:00–14:00). The aftermath has landed; the investigation begins — and the investigation *re-opens* something.

## Visual sequence
1. Chapter title that asks or overturns: "Why Did She Sink?", "The 2020 ROV and the Four-Metre Hole", "Chen Xing".
2. The new evidence shown, not told: the ROV footage, the warner's memo, the overlooked theory diagram.
3. 60–120 seconds of re-examination: the chain revisited through the new lens.
4. Resolution or honest uncertainty — then the reckoning proceeds.

## Typical duration
60–150 seconds. Long enough to matter, short enough to stay a beat.

## Motion strategy
Match the main video's grammar. The re-hook is a narrative device, not a visual one — no style change.

## Transition strategy
The chapter title is the re-hook's announcement. Hard cut into the new evidence.

## Sound strategy
[RESEARCH GAP.] Conservative: a slight lift — the re-hook is the one place a modest musical re-engagement is defensible. Never a sting.

## Common mistakes
- The re-hook that doesn't pay off (a question asked at 70% must be answered by 90%).
- Re-hook as cliffhanger-for-next-video (the Bible forbids unearned cliffhangers — the re-hook resolves *inside* this video).
- Placing the re-hook before the catastrophe (then it's just the structure, not a re-hook).
- The "shocking twist" framing (this is investigation, not a thriller — the tone stays documentary).

## Chain-of-Failure application
The re-hook is where the chain gets *re-examined*: the same links, seen through new evidence. It proves the chain wasn't simple — and that the investigation (the reckoning) is a real inquiry, not a victory lap. For our template, the re-hook sits between the catastrophe/aftermath and the reckoning, and it must always connect back to a link: the warner warned about *this* link; the new evidence reframes *that* decision.

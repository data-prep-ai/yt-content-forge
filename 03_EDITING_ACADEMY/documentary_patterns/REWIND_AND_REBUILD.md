# Pattern: REWIND_AND_REBUILD

## Purpose
The genre's default skeleton (90% of chapter-mapped videos [OBSERVED]): open at the catastrophe or verdict, rewind to origins, build the chain chronologically, return to the catastrophe, then investigate and close on legacy.

## When to use
Default structure for every episode. The 12–18 minute template maps our chain beats onto it directly.

## When NOT to use
- Countdown/real-time variant subjects: the escalation block becomes the ticking clock instead of a rewind (see REALTIME_COUNTDOWN).
- Genuine anthologies only (compilations) — and then each segment gets its own mini rewind.

## Narrative situation
The full video. Beats: cold open (dated paradox) → the confident system → link 1…n → the warnings → point of no return → catastrophe (brief) → reckoning → legacy → remembrance.

## Visual sequence
1. Cold-open image (single, legible).
2. Hard cut to the rewind: archival photos/maps of the confident system (the world before).
3. Annotated diagrams enter as the links begin (the "draw the failure" layer).
4. Timeline graphic fills as the chain tightens.
5. Mechanism cutaway at the hidden-mechanism beat (the visual climax).
6. Decelerated catastrophe: longer holds, fewer cuts.
7. Document close-ups for the reckoning (report pages).
8. Present-day imagery for the legacy close.

## Typical duration
Full 12–18 minute episode. Context block (confident system) capped at ~2 minutes — the observed drop-risk zone [INFERENCE from structure].

## Motion strategy
Ken Burns on archival (slow, directional); animated builds on diagrams; the mechanism cutaway is the only passage allowed continuous complex motion.

## Transition strategy
Chapter boundaries are the transitions. Hard cuts between chapters; each chapter title is a micro re-hook. No decorative transitions anywhere (see TRANSITION_RULES).

## Sound strategy
[RESEARCH GAP.] Conservative: low drone under escalation; silence at the catastrophe; sober resolved tone for legacy.

## Common mistakes
- Letting the context block exceed ~2–3 minutes without a crack (first warning or questionable decision) as the bridge — the observed risk zone is 26–30% background before the disaster.
- Placing the catastrophe late and the investigation early (inverts the genre grammar).
- Ending on the catastrophe instead of legacy + remembrance.

## Chain-of-Failure application
This pattern IS our format. Map every episode: confident system → links (each a named decision with a contemporaneous reason) → warnings (own titled beat) → point of no return (named on screen) → catastrophe (brief, decelerated) → reckoning → legacy (signature close).

# Pattern: WARNING_SEQUENCE

## Purpose
Give the ignored signals their own titled beat — never buried inside other sections (Bible §4.3). The warnings are the moral center of the chain: the catastrophe was knowable, and the edit must make the viewer feel that.

## When to use
Every episode. "Warnings ignored" is tied for the most recurrent psychology mechanism (5/20 [OBSERVED]). The warnings beat sits between the links and the escalation.

## When NOT to use
Never skip. If the record genuinely contains no documented warnings, say so on screen (uncertainty disclosure) — the absence of warnings is itself a finding.

## Narrative situation
Post-links, pre-escalation (template 6:00–10:00). The chain is built; now show that it was visible.

## Visual sequence
1. Chapter title: "The Warning" (or equivalent) — the beat is titled, not implied.
2. Each warning as a DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE beat: the memo, the reading, the inspection — one document, one push-in, one highlighted passage.
3. Between warnings: brief archival or diagram context (who received it, what they did).
4. The sequence accelerates: warnings come faster as the beat progresses (the edit performs the accumulation).
5. Final warning → hard cut to the point of no return.

## Typical duration
60–150 seconds for the full beat. Individual warnings: 15–30 seconds each. 2–4 warnings is the workable range.

## Motion strategy
Each warning gets the document push-in (stillness = credibility). The *sequence* accelerates through shorter holds and quicker cuts — the accumulation is felt in rhythm, not in camera moves.

## Transition strategy
Hard cuts between warnings. The final warning cuts directly to the point-of-no-return marker — no bridge, no narration buffer. The cut is the "and then it was too late."

## Sound strategy
[RESEARCH GAP.] Conservative: the warnings beat is quiet — the documents speak. A low drone may enter under the final warning as the escalation begins.

## Common mistakes
- Burying warnings inside the link beats (the Bible forbids this — the warnings must be their own titled beat).
- Listing warnings without showing the documents (a warning you can't see is a rumor).
- Equal weight to every warning (escalate: the final warning gets the longest hold and the hardest cut out).
- Warning-sign inflation: presenting ambiguous signals as clear warnings the record doesn't support (honesty gate).

## Chain-of-Failure application
This beat is the thesis made visible: the chain wasn't just causal, it was *visible*. Each warning is a link that could have broken the chain. The edit's job is to make the viewer feel the weight of "they knew" without ever saying "they were stupid" — the warnings were reasonable to dismiss at the time, and the documents must show why.

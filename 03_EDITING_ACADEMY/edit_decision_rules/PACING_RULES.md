# PACING_RULES — the rhythm of the edit

## The measured band
12 of 14 transcribed videos cluster at **9–16 caption segments/min with ~12 words per segment** [OBSERVED]. This is the genre's tightest measured cluster — treat it as the cadence target: roughly one visual idea per ~4–6 seconds, one breath-unit of narration per idea.

## Operational rules

- IF cutting a standard passage → THEN target ~12 visual ideas per minute (≈ one every 5 seconds). This is the genre's cruise cadence.
- IF the narration is dense with facts (specifications, numbers, names) → THEN slow the visual cadence, not the narration. Dense words need longer holds — the viewer reads the diagram while the numbers land.
- IF the beat is the catastrophe → THEN decelerate hard: the catastrophe is the slowest-cut passage in the video (Bible §8.3). Longer holds, fewer cuts. Spectacle is decelerated; the chain is accelerated.
- IF the beat is the chain tightening (warnings → escalation) → THEN accelerate: shorter holds, quicker cuts between warnings. The *rhythm* performs the trap closing.
- IF a context block runs past ~2 minutes → THEN re-escalate: place the technical warning-sign material immediately after the history section, exactly where attention would sag (the Sayano structure: history → "Rough Zone" → "Resonance" → "Fatigue" [OBSERVED]).
- IF the legacy close begins → THEN decelerate again. Sober resolution is slow.

## Pace as meaning (the three speeds)

1. **Chain speed** (cruise ~12/min): links, warnings, escalation. Forward motion.
2. **Catastrophe speed** (slow): the failure itself. Fewer cuts, longer holds, near-silence.
3. **Legacy speed** (slow, resolved): the reckoning and remembrance. The urgency is over.

## The AI-slop floor
Trace the Failure measured 26.2 segments/min at 5.4 words/segment with disclosed AI narration [OBSERVED]. Our pipeline must never produce choppy short-chunk cadence: minimum ~9 words per breath unit, maximum ~16 visual ideas per minute. Choppy segmentation is a measured credibility tell in this genre.

## Anti-patterns

- Uniform cadence throughout (a video cut at one speed for 15 minutes is a lecture, not a documentary — pace must carry meaning).
- Accelerating the catastrophe (fast cuts on the disaster = spectacle-mongering; the genre decelerates).
- Padding to a runtime (never pad; never compress a chain that needs room — Bible §8.5).
- The 26/5.4 pattern (see above — hard floor).

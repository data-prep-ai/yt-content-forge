# REHOOK_RULES — the mandatory final-third re-engagement

## The requirement
Every episode re-opens the central question at 65–80% runtime (Bible §4.5, gate #5). Not optional. Not accidental.

## The three forms (all observed — pick the one the record supports)

1. **Question restated** — "Why Did She Sink? — The Theories" (REF_003 at 80% [OBSERVED]). Use when the catastrophe's mechanism has genuine competing explanations.
2. **Reversal** — new evidence overturns the working theory: the 2020 ROV four-metre hole (REF_012 at 68% [OBSERVED]). Use when the record contains later evidence that reframes the story. Place it *after* the official story has settled.
3. **Vindicated warner** — the person who was right, placed late: Chen Xing at 85% (REF_006 [OBSERVED]). Use when the record contains a warning voice the story hasn't centered yet. The moral reversal.

## Operational rules

- IF the record contains competing theories → THEN form 1: restate the question as a chapter, spend 60–120 seconds on the theories, resolve or disclose honestly.
- IF the record contains later/overlooked evidence → THEN form 2: let the official story settle first, then drop the new evidence. The reversal needs the settled story as its setup.
- IF the record contains a warning voice not yet centered → THEN form 3: place the warner at 80–90%. The viewer re-experiences the whole chain through the warner's eyes.
- IF none of the three is supported → THEN the re-hook is the reckoning itself, framed as a question: "what did the investigation find that nobody expected?" Never manufacture a re-hook the record doesn't support.
- IF the re-hook asks a question → THEN it must be answered before the legacy close. A re-hook that doesn't pay off is a broken promise.

## Placement

- Window: 65–80% of runtime (REF_003: 80%; REF_012: 68%; REF_009: 65%; REF_006: 85% — the observed band).
- Structural position: post-catastrophe/aftermath, pre- or early-reckoning.
- Exactly one re-hook per video. Never stack.

## Anti-patterns

- The cliffhanger re-hook (a question designed to sell the *next* video — the Bible forbids unearned cliffhangers; the re-hook resolves inside this video).
- The "shocking twist" framing (investigation tone, not thriller tone).
- Re-hook before 60% (that's structure, not a re-hook).
- Two re-hooks (reads as the video not trusting its own story).

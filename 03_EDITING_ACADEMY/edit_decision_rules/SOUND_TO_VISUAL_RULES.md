## The principle
Music must never do the shouting the narrator refuses to do. The narration is restrained; the sound must be more restrained.

## Operational rules

- IF the visual is a photo move (Ken Burns) → THEN no SFX. Movement is not action — never sonify it.
- IF the visual is an annotation appearing → THEN no SFX. Marks are explanation, not events.
- IF the visual is a document beat → THEN silence or near-silence. Paper-rustle SFX is kitsch — never.
- IF the beat is the catastrophe → THEN music disappears. Silence or near-silence at the moment of failure (the "empty radar screen" beat is a silence-shaped moment [INFERRED from chapter structure]).
- IF the beat is the point of no return → THEN the sound thins to near-silence. The noise was the warnings; the silence is the inevitability.
- IF the toll is stated (anywhere) → THEN no stinger. Ever. The number lands flat, held, silent.
- IF the beat is the legacy/remembrance close → THEN a resolved, sober tone — never triumphant, never maudlin.
- IF the passage is escalation (warnings accumulating, chain tightening) → THEN a low drone may enter and build. The one place sound is allowed to *rise* — and it must stay low.
- IF music is present under narration → THEN the voice stays unambiguously forward. Music never competes with the words; duck it or cut it.

## When music should be present / reduced / absent

| Phase | Music |
|-------|-------|
| Cold open (dated paradox) | Near-silent or very low bed |
| Confident system | Low bed, neutral |
| Links | Low bed, neutral |
| Warnings | Quiet; low drone may enter under the final warning |
| Escalation | Low drone building (the one rising passage) |
| Point of no return | Thins to near-silence |
| Catastrophe | Absent — silence or near-silence |
| Aftermath | Near-silent; sober |
| Re-hook | Slight lift permitted (the one re-engagement) — never a sting |
| Reckoning | Low, sober |
| Legacy/remembrance | Resolved, sober — never triumphant, never maudlin |

## Anti-patterns (all banned)

- Stingers on death tolls (hard ban, Bible gate #9).
- Impact SFX on the catastrophe (the silence is the impact).
- Ticking-clock SFX under countdown chapters (kitsch — the chapters are the clock).
- Music that tells the viewer how to feel (the facts do that; the music stays out of the way).
- The triumphant swell on the legacy close (sober resolution, not victory).
- Any SFX on photo moves, annotation appearances, or map animation.

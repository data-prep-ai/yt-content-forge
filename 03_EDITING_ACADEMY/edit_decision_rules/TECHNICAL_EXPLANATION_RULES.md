# TECHNICAL_EXPLANATION_RULES — the mechanism passages

## The principle
Comprehension is the visual promise (Bible §7.1). The viewer should feel shown the *inside* of the failure. Technical passages are where the video earns its rewatch value and its "explain it to a friend" shareability.

## Operational rules

- IF the passage explains a mechanism → THEN the visual is a diagram or cutaway, never generic footage. The diagram carries the explanation; the narration labels it.
- IF the mechanism is a process over time → THEN the mechanism cutaway (once per video) with slowed narration. The Alertometer pairing: 134.6 WPM under continuous animation [OBSERVED] — slow voice, dense visual.
- IF the mechanism is a static relationship → THEN the annotated diagram, marks appearing in narration sync.
- IF the passage uses technical terms → THEN pick one register for the passage: technical-dense (Alertometer/USCSB/Hydraulic Record: "ram," "shear," "overtopping," "piping" [OBSERVED]) or plain-language (Fascinating Horror/Dark Records). Never mix within a paragraph (Bible §5.6).
- IF the term is an undecodable technical noun ("piping," "popcorn polymer," "free surface effect") → THEN define it visually the first time it appears. The noun is a curiosity asset — the diagram decodes it.
- IF the audience already understands the mechanism → THEN do not add unnecessary animation. One clear explanation per mechanism; repetition is clutter.

## The mechanism beat (placement)

- Position: ~25–40% runtime, after the links are established, before the escalation (Teton "Piping Failure" at 26% [OBSERVED]; Estonia "Free Surface Effect" at 33% inside the countdown [OBSERVED]).
- Duration: 45–120 seconds for the cutaway; 10–30 seconds per annotated diagram.
- This is the video's visual climax. Everything after it is consequence.

## Narration/visual synchronization

- The mark lands on the noun: circle appears as the part is named — never before, never after.
- Dense numbers → longer visual holds. The viewer reads the diagram while the numbers land.
- The mechanism beat is the one place the narration is *allowed* to slow below 145 WPM (the slow band: Alertometer 134.6, Salvage Room 137.2 [OBSERVED]).

## Anti-patterns

- Jargon without a visual anchor (a technical noun the screen doesn't show is a dropped thread).
- The unexplained cutaway (every moving part labeled or narrated).
- Two competing explanations without resolution (present the dispute honestly — see EVIDENCE_VISUAL_RULES — or pick the record's conclusion and say so).
- Mechanism as spectacle (dramatic camera orbits on the cutaway — the process is the motion, not the camera).

# MUSIC_RULES — the rule system for music in Chain-of-Failure documentaries

## Honesty header
[RESEARCH GAP.] The research environment had no video playback — actual music/sound design could not be observed in any video. This file is a **rule system built from the observed restraint norm** (1 exclamation mark across 14 transcripts; dramatic adjectives ≤1.6/1k in 10/14 [OBSERVED]), not a report of measured practice. Every rule below is conservative by design and marked EXPERIMENTAL in LEARNED_RULES until validated against real reference audio.

**Do not invent a music library.** No library is specified here. When a library is chosen, it must serve these rules — not the other way around.

## The governing principle
Music must never do the shouting the narrator refuses to do. Intensity comes from facts, not delivery — and not from the score.

## Music density
- **Default state: low or absent.** The genre's information density (info + explanation ≥65% [analyst classification]) means the voice carries the video. Music is a bed, never a feature.
- **One rising passage per video:** the escalation (warnings → point of no return). Everywhere else, music is flat or falling.
- **Maximum one musical "event" per video** (a lift at the re-hook, *or* a swell under the legacy — never both). Restraint is the brand.

## Voice/music relationship
- The voice is always unambiguously forward. Music ducks under narration or cuts out — never competes.
- During dense factual passages (specifications, numbers, report quotations), reduce music further or remove it. The viewer can't process numbers over a bed.
- During the mechanism cutaway: low drone only. The animation is dense; the voice is slow; the music must be nearly nothing.

## Phase-by-phase rules

### Hook (0:00–1:00 — dated paradox + stakes)
- Near-silent or very low neutral bed. The three flat sentences need room. The paradox is the retention device — music must not audition for the job.

### Investigation / links (the chain building)
- Low neutral bed or absent. The chain is interesting; the music is furniture.

### Uncertainty (disputed points, thin record)
- Music reduces or drops out. Uncertainty is stated plainly — a bed under a disclosure sounds like persuasion. Let the honesty be bare.

### Technical explanation (mechanism beat)
- Low drone or absent. The diagram explains; the voice labels; the music stays out of the way. This is the sparsest music in the video outside the catastrophe.

### Warning sequence
- Quiet through the early warnings. A low drone may enter under the *final* warning as the escalation begins — the one place dread is allowed to accumulate sonically.

### Rising danger / escalation
- The low drone builds. Slowly. This is the single rising passage — and "building" means a gradual thickening, not a crescendo. The trap closes; the sound barely leans in.

### Point of no return
- The sound thins to near-silence. The noise was the warnings; the silence is the inevitability. The contrast with the escalation drone is the effect.

### Catastrophe
- **Music absent.** Silence or near-silence at the moment of failure. The observed "empty radar screen" beat is a silence-shaped moment [INFERRED from chapter structure]. The catastrophe is decelerated visually — the sound follows.

### Aftermath
- Near-silent, sober. The toll is stated flat. No stingers on death tolls — ever (Bible gate #9, hard ban).

### Investigation / reckoning
- Low, sober bed returns. The inquiry is calm, procedural, unhurried.

### Legacy / remembrance
- A resolved, sober tone — never triumphant, never maudlin. The final line lands over the quietest music in the video, or none.

## Restraint rules (hard)

1. No stingers on death tolls. Ever.
2. No impact SFX on the catastrophe. The silence is the impact.
3. No ticking-clock SFX under countdown chapters. The chapters are the clock.
4. No music that tells the viewer how to feel (no "sad strings" on the toll, no "tension riser" on every warning — one riser per video, in the escalation, and even that stays low).
5. No triumphant swell on the legacy close. Sober resolution, not victory.
6. Music never competes with the voice. When in doubt, cut it.

## Avoiding over-dramatization
The test: mute the music and watch the passage. If the passage still works — facts, structure, visuals carrying it — the music was correctly restrained. If the passage *needs* the music to feel important, the passage is weak; fix the passage, not the mix. Music is never load-bearing in this genre.

## What to develop (open questions)
- Reference audio analysis: the first time real reference videos can be played, validate these rules against 3–5 Tier A references and promote/confine accordingly.
- The re-hook lift: is the "slight lift" at the re-hook real or should the re-hook also stay flat? Currently permitted conservatively; needs evidence.
- Library selection criteria: when choosing production music, the brief is "low neutral beds, one drone family, one resolved-close family" — three needs, not three hundred tracks.

# SOUND_DESIGN_RULES — SFX, silence, transitions, restraint

## Honesty header

## The governing principle
The soundtrack is voice first, silence second, music third, SFX almost never. Every sound must be defensible as *evidence* or *orientation* — never as thrill.

## SFX usage — the whitelist
SFX are permitted **only** in these cases:

1. **Documented primary audio** — the distress call, the cockpit recording, the warning siren *as recorded*. Primary audio is evidence, not effect. (Brick Immortar's in-medias-res distress-call open [INFERRED from description register] — the observed boundary case.)
2. **The mechanism's own sound, if documented** — e.g., the recorded "bang" (Meridian Zero's waveform thumbnail suggests acoustic evidence as a beat [INFERRED from thumbnail]). If the sound is in the record, it may be heard. If it isn't, it may not be invented.
3. **Functional UI sounds** — none. There are no UI sounds in this genre. (Stated to prevent invention.)

Everything else — whooshes, impacts, risers-as-SFX, paper rustles, footsteps, weather beds — is **banned**. The genre doesn't sonify its editing.

## Silence as a device
Silence is the most powerful sound in the toolkit. Scored silences:

- **The catastrophe:** music absent, SFX absent — silence or near-silence at the moment of failure.
- **The point of no return:** the drone thins to near-silence before the fall.
- **The empty-screen beat:** contact lost (the "Empty Radar Screen" chapter [OBSERVED]) — held, silent.
- **Document beats:** near-silence under the push-in. The paper speaks.
- **The toll:** stated flat over silence. No stinger, no bed swell, no SFX.

Rule: if a passage feels like it "needs" sound, check whether it needs *silence* first.

## Transitions (audio)
- Hard cuts are the audio default, matching the visual hard cuts.
- No transition SFX. Ever. (No whooshes between chapters — the chapter title is the transition.)
- Simple audio fades only where the visual fades (into the mechanism cutaway; out of the final image).
- The drone, where present, ducks under narration and cuts cleanly at beat boundaries — no long crossfades (crossfades blur the chapter grammar).

## Restraint checklist (pre-mix gate)
Before the mix ships, verify:
- [ ] No stingers on death tolls.
- [ ] No impact SFX on the catastrophe.
- [ ] No ticking-clock SFX.
- [ ] No whooshes on photo moves, annotation appearances, map animation, or chapter transitions.
- [ ] No Foley invented for events (no Hollywood creaks, explosions, or water sounds unless documented in the record).
- [ ] No paper-rustle, footstep, or weather-bed SFX.
- [ ] Music ducks under every dense factual passage.
- [ ] The catastrophe passage survives on silence.
- [ ] The legacy close is sober — not triumphant, not maudlin.

## Reconstruction audio
Disclosed reconstructions (see DISCLOSED_RECONSTRUCTION) get illustrative-or-silent audio — never designed to thrill. If the reconstruction shows the breach, the viewer hears near-silence or a low neutral bed, not a Hollywood dam-break. The label says "illustrative"; the sound must agree.

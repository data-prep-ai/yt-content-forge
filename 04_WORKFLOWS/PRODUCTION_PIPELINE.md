# FULL PRODUCTION PIPELINE

## 01 Discovery
Find candidate subjects. Score demand, evidence, narrative chain, visual potential, originality, legal/ethical risk, and production cost.

## 02 Validation
Reject subjects whose evidence is weak, too repetitive, too legally risky, or too difficult to explain visually.

## 03 Research
Build primary-source-first evidence pack. Record every important claim.

## 04 Story engineering
Build causal chain. Identify warnings, decisions, escalation, point of no return, failure, investigation, legacy.

## 05 Script
Write narration from the evidence. Do not write first and fact-check later.

## 06 Asset engineering
Translate narration into visual decisions. Evidence first. Then diagrams/maps. Then disclosed reconstructions. Stock is a stand-in, not fake evidence.

## 07 Asset acquisition/creation
Acquire or create only what the plan calls for. Record source and rights.

## 08 Voice
Generate or record consistent narration. Verify pronunciation and pacing.

## 09 Edit
Construct the timeline around causality and comprehension.

## 10 Sound/graphics
Voice first; restrained music; primary audio where valid; no invented drama. Add graphics and diagrams only when they teach.

## 11 QA
Check evidence, visuals, disclosure, audio, pacing, missing media, final render integrity.

## 12 Package
Title and thumbnail must promise what the video actually delivers.

## 13 Publish
Publish and store the final project metadata.

## 14 Learn
Capture analytics + postmortem. Update the OS using measured evidence.

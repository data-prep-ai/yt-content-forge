# ASSET TAXONOMY

## Evidence
- archival photo
- archival video
- official document
- map/documentary map
- primary audio
- investigation diagram

## Explanation
- mechanism diagram
- annotated photograph
- map route
- timeline
- data graphic
- cross-section / cutaway

## Reconstruction
- disclosed 2.5D reconstruction
- disclosed 3D reconstruction
- disclosed illustrative environment
- disclosed procedural animation

## Utility
- source cards
- lower thirds
- chapter/date cards
- typography
- safe-area overlays
- restrained transition helpers

## Audio
- music bed
- drone
- ambience where licensed and justified
- primary audio
- limited supporting SFX

## Priority order for each factual beat
Evidence > explanation graphic > disclosed reconstruction > honest stand-in > decoration

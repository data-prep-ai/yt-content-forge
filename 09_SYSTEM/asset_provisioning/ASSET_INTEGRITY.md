# ASSET INTEGRITY

For every retained asset, where practical record a SHA-256 hash in the registry.

The purpose is to detect accidental replacement, not to fingerprint or evade platform systems.

If an asset is replaced, preserve the old registry record and create a new asset ID.

Do not alter authentic evidence in a way that changes its factual meaning. Color correction, cropping, stabilization, or annotation must be logged where the change could affect interpretation.
